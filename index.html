02 / What SBFinance evaluates

Six views of SME exposure and resilience.

The current workflow covers the risk families and financial context below. It does not infer unsupported insurance lines or invent event probabilities.

Operational risk

Cyber disruption

Calculates the cost of a user-selected outage from daily fixed-cost burden, affected activity and stated recovery cost.

Concentration risk

Supply-chain interruption

Combines supplier-spend concentration with a selected interruption scenario, while requiring the user to confirm business criticality.

Physical risk

Asset / property loss

Models loss against the asset value supplied by the SME; asset value is never inferred from expense transactions.

Continuity risk

Business interruption

Models downtime, daily revenue, contribution margin and unavoidable fixed costs against policy limits and cash reserves.

Financial context

Financial resilience

Connects an uncovered event to available cash, runway, burn and the projected cash-gap date.

Lifecycle review

Growth-related protection changes

Re-runs supported exposures when revenue, operating dependency or stated assets change and flags limits that may no longer fit.

15 / FAQ

What to know before using the assessment.

The short version: SBFinance supports informed review. It does not sell insurance or replace a qualified professional.

What does SBFinance assess?

The public synthetic prototype illustrates how financial inputs, policy facts and a structured SME risk profile could support review of modelled exposure, possible coverage gaps and cash-flow impact. Production capabilities require pilot validation.

What do I need to provide?

The desktop workflow uses a CSV or XLSX financial export, a text-based insurance-policy PDF and a short structured risk profile. The public browser demo uses synthetic data and requires no upload.

Is the browser demo a real customer assessment?

No. The browser demo and sample report use a clearly labelled synthetic Singapore SME, an illustrative policy and precomputed deterministic scenarios.

Does my financial or policy data leave my machine?

The desktop analysis is designed for a local or private workflow so raw files do not need to be uploaded to a cloud-first analysis service. Optional external services, if configured, must be disclosed and validated for the deployment. The public demo does not upload financial exports or policy files.

Does SBFinance determine whether a claim is covered?

No. Possible coverage is indicative decision support, not a formal coverage determination. Actual coverage depends on complete policy wording, endorsements, exclusions and insurer determination. Verify policy interpretation with a qualified professional.

Are scenario losses actuarial predictions?

No. Scenario losses are transparent what-if calculations based on user-selected assumptions, not probabilities, forecasts or actuarial predictions.

How does SBFinance handle uncertainty or missing data?

Uncertain coverage is marked as uncertain, assumptions are shown, and missing information is surfaced rather than silently treated as zero. Policy interpretations require human verification.

What can a pilot include?

A pilot can include a financial export review, policy-fact extraction, selected disruption scenarios, an evidence-linked gap report and a feedback session. Scope is agreed before sensitive data is exchanged.

17 / Important limitations

Decision support has boundaries.

  • Synthetic demo data is not a real customer, policy, claim or insurer assessment.
  • Scenario losses are user-controlled what-if calculations, not actuarial predictions or event probabilities.
  • "Possible coverage" is not a formal coverage determination.
  • Actual coverage depends on complete wording, endorsements, exclusions and insurer determination.
  • Users should verify policy interpretation with a qualified insurance professional.
  • SBFinance is not an insurer, insurance broker, underwriter, legal adviser or claims handler.
  • The assessment is point-in-time and refreshes only when new financial, operational or policy data is supplied; it is not real-time monitoring.
  • The public proof is synthetic. Broad real-policy extraction validation and independent review of the scenario formulas remain incomplete.
